Threats, Vulnerabilities and Risk

Understanding the Security Chain

A threat is an event that may cause harm; a vulnerability is a weakness that can be exploited; risk combines the likelihood of an event with its impact. In college life, typical examples include accounts without MFA, unpatched software, or oversharing details publicly.

Source: NIST, Cybersecurity Basics (2025)
